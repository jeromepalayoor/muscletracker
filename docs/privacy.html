Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Muscle Tracker does not collect, transmit, retain or share any of your data.

Data Storage

Everything you enter — templates, workout sessions, sets, weights, reps — is stored only in a local database on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, because this app doesn't have one.

Accounts & Tracking

  • There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no analytics.
  • The app does not contain any advertisements or third-party tracking of any kind.

Exporting Data

The only way your data ever leaves your device is if you choose to use the Export feature in Settings, which lets you save a backup file yourself. That file goes wherever you choose to save it — it is never sent anywhere automatically.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything, since nothing exists outside of it.

Contact
